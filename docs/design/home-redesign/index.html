Round two: four directions for the CraftsAI home page

Each one opens with a different idea of what an AI studio is. Open each, watch the first ten seconds, move your mouse over the hero, then scroll. Tell me which one to build (or which parts to combine).

D. The Rack

The whole page is one piece of AI hardware. It powers on, modules slot in, and you drag patch cables between services while the screen describes the system you just wired. Examples are cartridges you plug in.

E. X-Ray

Every part of the page exists twice: what a person reads, and what the AI sees. A scan bar reads the page first, then your cursor becomes a lens into the machine layer. The button at bottom right flips the whole page.

F. The Descent

No sections. The headline forms out of light, and scrolling flies you through the layers of a neural network, one chapter per layer, ending at the bright output core.

A. Attention

A model writes the headline in front of you, token by token. Then attention arcs link the words; hover any word to see what it attends to.
