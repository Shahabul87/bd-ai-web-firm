Principles

  • Measured, not guessed

    Every system ships with evaluations you can read.

  • Your data stays yours

    Private deployments, no training on your data without consent.

  • Model-agnostic

    We pick the model that fits the job, and can switch when a better one arrives.

  • People in the loop

    The AI handles volume; your team keeps the judgement calls.
