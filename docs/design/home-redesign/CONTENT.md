## Services (six; "AI agents" is the flagship)
1. AI agents & automation — Agents that plan and act inside your tools: they read tickets, update the CRM, draft replies, and hand off to a person when it matters.
2. Assistants on your data — Chat and search over your documents, databases, and wikis, with answers that cite their sources.
3. Custom models & fine-tuning — When an off-the-shelf model isn't enough, we train, fine-tune, and distil models for your task and your budget.
4. AI inside your product — We add AI features to the web and mobile apps you already have, or build new AI-first products end to end.
5. Data & evaluation pipelines — Clean data in, measurable quality out: datasets, test suites, and evals that tell you whether the AI is actually getting better.
6. AI strategy sprint — Two weeks to find where AI pays off in your business, with a working prototype at the end, not a slide deck.

## What we build (use cases, not case studies — label as examples)

## How we work (a real sequence)
1. Discover — We map the workflow, the data, and what "good" looks like.
2. Prototype — A working demo on your real data within weeks.
3. Evaluate — We measure accuracy, cost, and speed against the bar we agreed.
4. Build — Production-grade integration, security, and monitoring.
5. Run & improve — We watch it in production and keep making it better.

## Principles (how we're different)
- Measured, not guessed — every system ships with evaluations you can read.
- Your data stays yours — private deployments, no training on your data without consent.
- Model-agnostic — we pick the model that fits the job, and can switch when a better one arrives.
- People in the loop — the AI handles volume; your team keeps the judgement calls.
