    How we're different

    Four rules we hold ourselves to on every engagement.

    • Measured, not guessed

      Every system ships with evaluations you can read.

    • Your data stays yours

      Private deployments, no training on your data without consent.

    • Model-agnostic

      We pick the model that fits the job, and can switch when a better one arrives.

    • People in the loop

      The AI handles volume; your team keeps the judgement calls.

    How we work

    The same five steps, in this order, whatever we build.

    1. Discover

      We map the workflow, the data, and what “good” looks like.

    2. Prototype

      A working demo on your real data within weeks.

    3. Evaluate

      We measure accuracy, cost, and speed against the bar we agreed.

    4. Build

      Production-grade integration, security, and monitoring.

    5. Run & improve

      We watch it in production and keep making it better.
